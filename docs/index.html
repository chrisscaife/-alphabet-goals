How to play

  1. Start a game and send the link to your mates. Everyone types their name and joins. Anyone can kick off.
  2. Each turn the game shows a letter and a position, e.g. U · Defender. Name a player whose surname starts with that letter, like Unsworth.
  3. Goals are revealed after you submit, so nobody can look them up first. They go on your score.
  4. No repeats. Once a player has been named, nobody can use him again. Stuck? Pass, or skip the letter for everyone.
  5. Typos are fine. The game asks "Did you mean…?" when you're close.

Game modes

  • Most goals wins uses goals in England's top division since 1992.
  • Fewest goals wins: lowest total wins, but a player who never scored, or a pass, costs 15.
  • Goalkeepers scores clean sheets.
  • Two letters: Europe: match a first-name and surname letter. 10 points plus career goals, assists or clean sheets.
  • International edition: players from every national team. Forwards score international goals; everyone else scores international caps. Play it with one letter or two.

Stats come from public football records up to the start of the 2026/27 season. Some older players' numbers are estimates and are marked as such. Not affiliated with any league or club.
