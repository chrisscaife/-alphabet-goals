Privacy Policy

Alphabet Goals · Last updated 10 November 2026

Alphabet Goals is a football quiz game you play with friends. It is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores

  • The name you type to join a game. It is shown to the other people in that game.
  • A random ID for your device, made up by the app. It is not linked to your phone number, email, Apple ID or Google account, and is only used to know which seat in a game is yours.
  • The game itself: who is playing, the players you name, the scores and whose turn it is.

Your name and device ID are also saved on your own phone so you don't have to type your name each time.

Where it is kept

Games are stored on Google Firebase servers so everyone in the game sees moves straight away. Each game has its own private, randomly generated link. Only people with the link can open that game. Firebase signs each phone in anonymously; no personal details are involved.

Optional turn alerts

If you choose to turn on lock-screen alerts, the game sends a short "your turn" message to the free ntfy service (ntfy.sh), which passes it to the ntfy app on your phone. The message only contains the game name and the letter for your turn. You don't have to use this.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't show adverts or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or any other data from your phone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It is not designed specifically for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them beyond a first name typed into a game.

Deleting your data

You can remove yourself from a game's squad at any time with the Leave button. To have a game or your name removed from our servers, contact us at the address below with the game link and we'll delete it.
