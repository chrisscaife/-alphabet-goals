Help

Common questions

How do my friends join?

Start a new game, then tap WhatsApp, Share link or Copy link. Anyone who opens the link can type their name and join, in the app or in any phone browser.

A player I named scored more than the game says

Stats run to the start of the 2026/27 season, so goals scored since then may not be counted yet. Some older players' totals are estimates and say so when revealed. If a number looks wrong, email us the player's name and we'll check it.

The game says it can't find a player

Check the spelling. The game offers "Did you mean…?" when you're close. Very obscure players may not be in our list; you can pass instead.

My friend can't see my move

Check the dot at the top says "Live". If it says "Connecting", the phone has lost signal; moves will send when it's back. Closing and reopening the game also reconnects it.

How do I get a "your turn" alert?

In a game, open 🔔 Turn alerts at the bottom and tap Turn on alerts. For alerts on your lock screen when the game is closed, follow the ntfy steps in the same box.

Does it cost anything or show adverts?

No. It's free, with no adverts and no in-app purchases.
